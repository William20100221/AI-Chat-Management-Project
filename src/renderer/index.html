    Welcome to AI Chat Manager

    A sticky note for your AI chats. It finds the AI apps and websites you use (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and more) and shows which chats need you: the AI is replying, has replied, or is asking you something.

    Everything stays on this computer. The app only reads; it never changes the AI apps' files and never sends anything anywhere.

    What may it read?

    You can change these any time in Settings.

    On a Mac: macOS will ask whether AI Chat Manager may open your Downloads folder. Click Allow for exports to be imported.

    Safari's history needs Full Disk Access: turn on AI Chat Manager in the list that opens. Other browsers don't need this.

    Live chats from AI websites (optional)

    With the browser extension, the app also shows when a website's AI is replying, new replies, and your questions. Browsers don't let apps install extensions by themselves, so it takes a few clicks:

    1. Click Open extension folder below.
    2. In your browser, open the extensions page (Copy address, then paste it into the address bar), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose that folder.
    3. Open or reload your AI websites. That's it.

    Firefox: about:debugging → This Firefox → Load Temporary Add-on → choose manifest.json in that folder. You can also do all this later from Settings → Connections.

    All set

    Your AI chats will appear in a moment.

    Tip: the arrows button at the top shrinks the window to a narrow list of titles, like a sticky note.

      Connections

      The app looks for AI apps on this computer first, then for AI chat pages in your browser history. For live replies on AI websites, it links to a small browser extension.

      1. 1
        AI apps on this computer
        Claude Desktop (Cowork, Code), Claude Code, ChatGPT app (Work, Codex), Codex CLI, Gemini CLI
        Checking…
      2. 2
        Browser history
        Chat pages on any AI website: titles and when you opened them. Edge, Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Arc, Firefox, Safari.
        Checking…

        Only addresses on AI websites are read, nothing else from your history. Nothing leaves this computer.

      3. 3
        Live from AI websites (browser extension)
        Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok, Le Chat and Poe: chat titles, your questions, replying now and new replies
        Checking…
        1. Edge or Chrome: open edge://extensions (or chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
        2. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json in the extension folder. (Firefox removes it when it restarts.)
        3. Open any AI website (or reload it). This turns green within a minute.

        The extension only reads what those tabs already loaded, and sends it to this app on your computer, nowhere else.
