    Connections

    The app looks for Claude on this computer first. For claude.ai in your browser, it links to a small browser extension.

    1. 1
      Claude on this computer
      Checking…
    2. 2
      Claude website (claude.ai)
      Checking…
      1. Edge or Chrome: open edge://extensions (or chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
      2. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json in the extension folder. (Firefox removes it when it restarts.)
      3. Open claude.ai (or reload it). This turns green within a minute.

      The extension only reads what your claude.ai tab already loaded, and sends it to this app on your computer, nowhere else.

    For chats from before you installed the extension, use your data export: in Claude go to Settings → Privacy → Export data and download the file from the email. This app spots it in Downloads and helps you fetch your chats.
