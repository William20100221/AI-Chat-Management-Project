      Connections

      The app looks for AI apps on this computer first, then for AI chat pages in your browser history. For live replies on AI websites, it links to a small browser extension.

      1. 1
        AI apps on this computer
        Claude Desktop (Cowork, Code), Claude Code, ChatGPT app (Work, Codex), Codex CLI, Gemini CLI
        Checking…
      2. 2
        Browser history
        Chat pages on any AI website: titles and when you opened them. Edge, Chrome, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Arc, Firefox, Safari.
        Checking…

        Only addresses on AI websites are read, nothing else from your history. Nothing leaves this computer.

      3. 3
        Live from AI websites (browser extension)
        Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok, Le Chat and Poe: chat titles, your questions, replying now and new replies
        Checking…
        1. Edge or Chrome: open edge://extensions (or chrome://extensions), turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked and choose the extension folder.
        2. Firefox: open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox, click Load Temporary Add-on and choose manifest.json in the extension folder. (Firefox removes it when it restarts.)
        3. Open any AI website (or reload it). This turns green within a minute.

        The extension only reads what those tabs already loaded, and sends it to this app on your computer, nowhere else.

      Chat history

      For chats from before you installed the extension, use each platform's data export. Download the file from the email, and this app spots it in Downloads and imports it.

      • Claude: Settings → Privacy → Export data
      • ChatGPT: Settings → Data controls → Export data
