    How chats get in

    Cowork tasks and Code sessions update on their own, including whether Claude is replying. Normal chats live on Claude's servers, so they come from your data export: in Claude go to Settings → Privacy → Export data, then download the file from the email. This app spots it in Downloads and helps you fetch your chats.
